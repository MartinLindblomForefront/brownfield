# Seed bank glossary

These definitions are a starting point for the training project; a seed-bank
specialist should review them as the project develops.

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Accession | A distinct addition to the collection, with its own identifier and provenance. |
| Provenance | Information about where material came from and how it was obtained. |
| Seed lot | A quantity of seeds tracked as a group, often associated with an accession. |
| Viability | The ability of seeds to germinate under suitable conditions. |
| Regeneration | Growing plants to produce fresh seeds for the collection. |
| Safety duplicate | A backup sample held separately to reduce the risk of loss. |
