# E-4: Regenerate seed collections

Manage growing plants from stored seeds to replenish a collection when stock
or viability runs low.

## Potential features

- F-13: Regeneration planning
- F-14: Grow-out records
- F-15: Harvest registration

## Candidate KPIs

- **Regeneration coverage:** percentage of accessions identified as needing
  regeneration that have an approved regeneration plan.
- **Regeneration completion:** percentage of regeneration projects completed
  during the reporting period that produced an accepted seed lot meeting
  staff-defined quantity and quality criteria.
