# E-5: Manage requests and distribution

Support requests for seeds while keeping a record of what was supplied and to
whom.

## Potential features

- F-16: Request review
- F-17: Availability checks
- F-18: Dispatch and distribution history
- F-19: Cost-recovery fee records

## Candidate KPIs

- **Eligible request fulfillment:** percentage of eligible requests closed
  during the reporting period that were fulfilled, fully or partially; report
  full and partial fulfillment separately.
- **Time to decision:** median time from receipt to decision for requests
  decided during the reporting period.
- **Reach:** number of distinct recipient organizations receiving seeds during
  the reporting period, optionally broken down by recipient type or region.
- **Distribution cost recovery:** earned distribution-service fees divided by
  the full cost of fulfilling distribution requests during the reporting period,
  including subsidized requests. Track this alongside reach and fulfillment so
  cost recovery does not overshadow access.
