# E-1: Register and describe seeds

Record what seeds the bank holds, where they came from, and their identifying
characteristics.

## Potential features

- [F-1: Register an accession](features/register-an-accession.md): Create a
  collection record with a unique identifier and basic identifying information.
- [F-2: Describe the plant](features/describe-the-plant.md): Record the scientific
  name and other known plant identification details, while allowing uncertain
  or unknown details to be documented.
- [F-3: Record provenance](features/record-provenance.md): Capture known collection
  details, such as where and when the material was collected, and its source
  or donor.
- [F-4: Find and view accessions](features/find-and-view-accessions.md): Browse and
  search the catalogue and view an accession's recorded details.
- [F-5: Maintain accession records](features/maintain-accession-records.md): Update
  details as information becomes available or corrections are made.
- [F-6: Review record completeness](features/review-record-completeness.md):
  Identify accessions missing locally required identification or provenance
  information so staff can follow up.

A first delivery could combine basic registration, a plant name and known
provenance with the ability to find and view the new record. The required
fields and taxonomy conventions need domain review. The enabling story US-14
in F-1 establishes the development foundation before US-1.

## Candidate KPIs

- **Registration completeness:** percentage of active accessions with all
  locally required identification and provenance fields recorded.
- **Documented diversity:** number of distinct taxa represented by active
  accessions, reported alongside the number of active accessions. Interpret
  changes in light of the bank's conservation priorities.
