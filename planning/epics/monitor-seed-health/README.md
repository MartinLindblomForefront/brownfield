# E-3: Monitor seed health

Track whether stored seeds are still likely to germinate and identify those
needing attention.

## Potential features

- F-10: Viability-test records
- F-11: Testing schedules
- F-12: Follow-up alerts

## Candidate KPIs

- **Current viability information:** percentage of active accessions with a
  viability test recorded within their staff-defined testing interval.
- **Viability concerns addressed:** percentage of accessions identified during
  the reporting period as below their staff-defined viability threshold that
  have a documented follow-up decision.
