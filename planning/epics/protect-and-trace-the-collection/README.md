# E-6: Protect and trace the collection

Reduce the risk of losing material and make important changes traceable.

## Potential features

- F-20: Safety-duplicate records
- F-21: Audit history
- F-22: Reports on collections needing attention

## Candidate KPIs

- **Safety-duplication coverage:** percentage of active accessions selected
  for safety duplication that have a confirmed duplicate held separately.
- **Traceability:** percentage of seed lots sampled for review during the
  reporting period for which their accession, storage location, and recorded
  movements can be traced.
