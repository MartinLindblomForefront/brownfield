# E-2: Track inventory and storage

Know how much seed is available and where it is stored.

## Potential features

- F-7: Seed-lot inventory
- F-8: Storage locations
- F-9: Stock movements and low-stock alerts

## Candidate KPIs

- **Inventory accuracy:** percentage of seed lots physically checked during the
  reporting period whose quantity and location match the records.
- **Low-stock exposure:** percentage of active accessions below their
  staff-defined minimum available seed quantity.
